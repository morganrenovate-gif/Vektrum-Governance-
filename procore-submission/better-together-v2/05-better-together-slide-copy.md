**The Joint Solution (outcomes, ≤6 words each):**

## SLIDE 4 – Real-World Use Cases

**Use case 1 — Change-order-aware draw readiness**
When an open change order touches a draw's milestone, the release gate blocks that authorization until it is resolved. In the simulated joint workflow, resolving the change order at source clears the block. [V-09, J-01]

**Use case 2 — Lien-waiver completeness before release**
When the required conditional lien waiver is not yet approved, the gate blocks the draw; when the approved waiver is on file, the gate can pass and the funder can authorize. [V-09, V-02]

**Use case 3 — Milestone isolation with execution proof**
When one milestone is disputed, only that release unit is blocked — unrelated milestones keep moving. When the title or escrow partner executes an authorized release, its confirmation is recorded against the signed authorization. [V-08, V-10, V-11]
